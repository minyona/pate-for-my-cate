# Paté for my Caté — Website Image Prompts

Brand vibe: 1990s Viennetta luxury — deep navy and burgundy backgrounds, antique gold flourishes, soft warm side-lighting, marble surfaces, velvet drape, slightly cinematic film grain. Photoreal, editorial, French luxury catalog energy from a 1995 Sunday-paper insert. Approachable, not stuffy.

Color anchors that should appear across the set so the website feels cohesive: midnight navy `#0a1628`, deep burgundy `#5c1f2e`, antique gold `#c9a961`, warm cream `#f5ebd6`.

---

## 1. Hero — Le Chat Royal
**Aspect:** 3:2 landscape
**Use:** Top-of-page hero image, right side of the fold next to the script logo.

Photoreal editorial product portrait shot on medium-format film, 1995 luxury food advertising aesthetic, soft film grain. A single regal long-haired silver Persian cat sits in three-quarter profile on a swath of crushed midnight-navy velvet, head turned slightly toward camera, eyes half-closed with serene confidence. Directly in front of the cat sits one small unopened cat-food tin: matte cream label, ornate antique-gold script type, a tiny gold foil seal at the top. The tin reads "Paté for my Caté" in elegant italic script (both é's accented). Beside the tin: a single sprig of fresh parsley and one drip of golden olive oil on the velvet. Background: out-of-focus deep burgundy drapery with a faint gold damask pattern, lit by warm window light from camera-left, slight rim light on the cat's whiskers. Shallow depth of field, f/2.8, 85mm equivalent. Mood: refined, expensive, French, 1990s perfume-ad calm. No people. No text overlays beyond the label on the tin.

**Use:** Product showcase section — three flavors arranged on a counter.

Lit by warm 4pm window light from camera-right, soft shadow falling left. Slight film grain. 50mm equivalent, f/4. No people. No additional text or logos.

## 3. Le Dîner — The Cat Dining
**Aspect:** 4:5 portrait
**Use:** Lifestyle / "your cat will purr" section.

Photoreal editorial lifestyle shot, 1995 luxury pet-food magazine ad aesthetic, soft film grain. A plump short-haired Russian Blue cat eats delicately from a small bone-china dessert dish rimmed in antique gold, dish set on a dark walnut antique dining table. The cat's pink tongue is mid-lick, eyes nearly closed in contentment. The portion in the dish is a glossy mound of paté, garnished with one tiny parsley leaf. Beside the dish: a folded burgundy linen napkin with a single gold-thread monogrammed "P," a small unopened tin of "Paté for my Caté" with cream label and gold script, and one brass candlestick with a softly burning candle just out of frame top-left throwing warm flicker light. Background: deep midnight-navy dining room wall, blurred gold-framed portrait of a cat (oil painting style) hanging on the wall behind. Camera angle: low, table-level, intimate. 35mm equivalent, f/2.5. Mood: serene, indulgent, slightly cinematic. No people. No text overlays.

## 5. L'Atelier — The Kitchen
**Aspect:** 16:9 landscape
**Use:** Brand story / "Une Histoire" section behind the founder copy.

Photoreal editorial interior shot, 1995 Provençal kitchen at golden hour, slight film grain. An empty corner of a French country kitchen: cream-veined marble counter running along the bottom third of the frame, a row of polished copper pots and a small copper saucepan hanging from a wrought-iron rail above. On the counter: a wooden cutting board with a half-chopped shallot and a small chef's knife resting beside it, a sprig of fresh thyme, a folded burgundy linen apron embroidered in gold thread with a tiny cursive "P" hanging off the counter edge, one small tin of "Paté for my Caté" upright next to the cutting board. Background wall: aged cream plaster with a single small framed antique illustration of a cat in profile, deep midnight-navy wainscoting along the bottom of the wall. Window just out of frame on the right pouring warm 5pm golden light across the marble, soft shadow of the hanging copper pots falling across the wall. No people. No modern appliances visible. Quiet, lived-in, expensive but warm. 35mm equivalent, f/4.
