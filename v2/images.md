# Paté for my Caté — V2 Image Prompts (Snap-Pack + Le Comte)

**Brand direction for v2:** still Viennetta-1990s (deep navy, burgundy, antique gold, cream, ornate script, soft film grain) — BUT we've swapped the can for a modern plastic single-serve snap-pack tray with a peel-off foil lid, and we've added a mascot: **Le Comte de Whiskersworth III**, a chubby black-and-white tuxedo cat in a tiny burgundy velvet bowtie. The tone is *Lifestyles of the Rich and Famous* (Robin Leach voiceover energy): pompous, knowingly absurd, fourth-wall-breaking — funny to a Gen-X parent in their 40s. Still photoreal product photography, still serious about food quality. The humor lives in the staging, not the lighting.

**Mascot anchors** (must be consistent across all images that include him):
- Chubby black-and-white tuxedo shorthair, age ~5, slight smug-confident expression
- Tiny burgundy velvet bowtie at his throat (gold buckle accent)
- Same cat across all prompts — same markings, same proportions

**Packaging anchors** (must be consistent across all images):
- Modern plastic single-serve cat-food snap-pack tray (~3" x 4", oval or rounded-rectangle)
- Tray itself: matte cream-colored plastic with a thin antique-gold rim around the top edge
- Foil lid: brushed cream foil printed with ornate antique-gold italic script reading **"Paté for my Caté"** across the top (both é's accented), with the flavor name and "No. 0X" beneath in smaller gold script, and a tiny gold foil rosette in the lower-right corner of the foil
- Foil pull-tab in the front-center
- Where shown peeled back: glossy mound of paté visible inside, garnished with the flavor's signature herb/ingredient

**Color anchors (all five):** midnight navy `#0a1628`, deep burgundy `#5c1f2e`, antique gold `#c9a961`, warm cream `#f5ebd6`.
