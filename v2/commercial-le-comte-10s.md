# Le Comte's Standards — 10-second i2v Commercial

**Source image:** `v2/images/image-01.jpg` (hero — Le Comte in velvet wingback with snap-pack on gold side table, mimosa, gilt mirrors, chandelier).
**Target:** Veo 3 / Sora / Kling / any modern i2v with native voiceover.
**Length:** 10.0s.

---

## Style anchors
A 1986 *Lifestyles of the Rich and Famous* parody, applied to a $1.89 cat-food snap-pack. Photoreal. Slight 35mm film grain. Warm candle flicker + soft window side-light from the right. Dignified, deadpan, slow. Comedy lives in stillness, not motion. Treat the cat like Bond, not like a meme.

---

## Beats

### Beat 1 — 0.0–2.5s · "Establish"
Slow, smooth dolly-in (~8–10% push). Wall-sconce candles flicker subtly (top-left). Faint chandelier prisms shift a hair as if the room breathed. The cat sits perfectly still. At ~2.0s he turns his head ONE INCH toward camera and gives a slow, slightly-disdainful blink. A single column of small bubbles rises lazily through the mimosa.

### Beat 2 — 2.5–5.0s · "The Gesture"
Le Comte's white front-right paw lifts off the velvet cushion with the slowest, most disinterested authority — a single deliberate gesture in the direction of the open snap-pack, as if dismissing a butler. The corner of the peeled foil catches the light. His chest rises once with a slow breath.

### Beat 3 — 5.0–8.0s · "Bond-Villain Blink"
Dolly continues; framing tightens slightly on chest, bowtie, snap-pack. Le Comte locks half-lidded eye contact with the lens and delivers one slow, theatrical blink. Candles flicker on cue. A near-imperceptible whisker twitch.

### Beat 4 — 8.0–10.0s · "Resolve"
Camera holds. Le Comte slowly turns his head a fraction further and looks past the camera, ignoring it entirely. A soft exhale through the nose. The wordmark **Paté for my Caté** fades up in ornate antique-gold italic script across the lower third (cream color, both é's accented). Beneath it, in smaller script: *"— $1.89, darling. —"*

## Audio

**Voiceover** — male, calm, theatrical, deeply self-amused. Robin Leach *restrained*, NOT shouted. Warm baritone. Slight British inflection at "darling." Spoken slowly, with pauses.

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0.0 – 3.0s | "He's never paid for a thing in his life…" |
| 3.0 – 5.5s | "…and yet, somehow — he has standards." |
| 5.5 – 7.0s | "Paté for my Caté." |
| 7.0 – 10.0s | "Real meat. Real French. Real… one dollar eighty-nine." |

**Score** — Soft solo violin pizzicato over light harp arpeggios. Baroque-lite, 1990s-perfume-commercial energy. Sits low, beneath the VO. Resolves into one sustained harp note at 9.5s.

**Foley**
- ~1.5s — faint candle crackle
- ~3.2s — subtle foil-flutter rustle (snap-pack lid corner)
- ~4.0s — one delicate metallic "tink" off the mimosa flute rim
- ~6.5s — slow exhale through nose
- ~9.5s — gentle harp resolve, room tone

No laugh tracks. No sting. No cartoon SFX. No music swell on the price.
