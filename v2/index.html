02.A Profile, in his own words

An audience
with the Comte.

Le Comte de Whiskersworth III was born in a sunbeam on a stack of Architectural Digest, May 1991. He has never worked a day in his life. He has, however, developed strong opinions about cat food, and we have agreed to publish them.

He insists on real meat. He refuses anything described as a “by-product.” He will not eat from a can — the opener offends him personally. The snap-pack was his idea. The pricing was ours.

He is not for sale. The paté is. Adjacent.

03.The Snap-Pack

One tray.
One foil. One small woman in Lyon.

  1. i.

    Peel.

    A small foil pull-tab. The kind that makes a sound. Cats hear this sound from three rooms away.

  2. ii.

    Serve.

    Into a small dish, or, if you must, present it on the tray with both hands and a small bow.

  3. iii.

    Witness gratitude.

    Or don't. Cats are not contractually obligated to thank you. Smug eye contact is statistically likely.

Recyclable tray. Biodegradable foil. The same five real ingredients we started with, in the same kitchen, by the same exhausted woman.

08.Particulars

Questions you may have.

If yours isn't here, write to us. We answer everything, eventually, in the order Le Comte permits.

When does my box ship?
Within 48 hours of order, on the same day Tuesday through Friday. Trays are hand-spooned the morning of shipment, then packed in a small insulated mailer. Most US orders arrive in 2–4 business days.
Can I pause or skip a month?
Yes. One click from your account or the confirmation email. Pause, skip a month, swap flavors, or cancel entirely. Le Comte will not be informed.
What if my cat refuses it?
The first tray is on us — full refund, no return. Keep the rest of the box to befriend the local strays. Roughly four percent of cats refuse on first contact and accept on second; we send a small note explaining this to your cat.
Is this real food, or is it a bit?
Both. The salmon is wild-caught Atlantic. The chicken is free-range from a small farm in Vermont. The “one small woman in Lyon” is a generalization — it's actually a kitchen of seven people. The bit is funny. The food is serious.
Why $1.89 per tray?
Because we cut the marketing budget. There is no celebrity-cat endorsement deal, no shelf-placement payment to retailers, no agency on retainer. The savings are passed directly to you. You're welcome.
