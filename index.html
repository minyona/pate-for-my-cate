Une Histoire

For Mademoiselle — and every cat after her.

It started, of course, with a cat. A small grey Russian Blue named Mademoiselle who refused to eat anything from a can that didn't taste like food. The shelf at the grocery store was full of $0.79 mystery and $5.99 boutique. There was nothing in the middle.

So we built the middle. A proper paté — real meat first, no fillers, no marketing math — that costs what cat food should cost. We named it the only way a French cat would have it.
